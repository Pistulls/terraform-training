What this training is

The course follows the order of HashiCorp's own learning path: Infrastructure as Code, then providers, versions and the lock file, then the core workflow with every init, fmt, validate, plan, apply and destroy option. From there it covers configuration, modules, state and HCP Terraform.

  • Lesson pages teach one point, with a short example and an On the exam note where there's a common trap.
  • Checkpoints are 4 to 6 questions. Each answer shows why it's right, with the doc to go back to.
  • The counter reads like a Terraform plan: lessons to learn and learned. A lesson counts as learned when you move past it; a checkpoint when every question is answered.
  • Topics the learning path leaves out but the exam commonly tests, such as provider aliases and CLI workspaces, are folded in where they fit.
  • A Platform specifics unit gathers details from the AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle, Docker and Kubernetes tutorials that practice tests sometimes ask about.

Already confident on objectives 1 to 3? Open the contents list in the course and jump to Data sources and references; the early checkpoints are a quick way to confirm.

The certification it targets

HashiCorp Certified: Terraform Associate (004), the entry-level Terraform certification. It tests the concepts and skills of working with Terraform CLI and HCP Terraform, against Terraform 1.12.

The numbers beside each unit above are the exam objectives it covers, from the official exam content list. When you're ready, book the exam from the HashiCorp certifications page.

How your progress is stored

  • Everything is kept in your browser's localStorage, under the key tf004-course-v1. There is no account, server or tracking.
  • It records which pages you've learned, your answer to each checkpoint question, and the page you were last on, so the course reopens where you left off.
  • Progress belongs to this browser on this device. Another browser, another device or a private window starts fresh.
  • Clearing site data for this domain erases it. If storage is blocked, the course still works but forgets your progress when you close the tab.
  • To start over, use Reset progress at the foot of any course page. To redo one quiz, use its Retake link.

Source material

The course was written with Claude as an interactive study artifact, then turned into this site. Its content is drawn from HashiCorp's official material:

  • The Terraform Associate 004 learning path and exam content list.
  • The Terraform language and CLI docs, pinned to v1.12 where a versioned page exists, and the HCP Terraform docs.
  • The AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle, Docker and Kubernetes get-started tutorials, for the Platform specifics unit.

Every lesson links to the pages it's based on. Check the official sample questions for the exam's question style.
